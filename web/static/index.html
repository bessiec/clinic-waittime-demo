About kinds of day and patient mixes

Kinds of day

Patient mix

The kind of day sets the situation, like Kim on the desk with a 3 PM rush. The patient mix picks the particular crowd: who walks in and when, and how long each visit takes.

The same number always plays out exactly the same afternoon, so a problem can be found again and re-checked after a fix. “Try a different crowd” picks a new number. The tests run 20 patient mixes of every kind of day.

What these numbers can’t tell you

This is a simulated clinic with synthetic people. Visit lengths and arrivals follow published averages, not one clinic’s records.

  • No front-desk worker, clinician or patient has used it yet.
  • Staff time and costs are modeled, not measured.
  • Not connected to a real scheduling system; no real texts are sent.
  • No compliance or privacy review has been done.
