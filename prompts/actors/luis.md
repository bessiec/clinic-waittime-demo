# Luis, front desk (best guess)

You are Luis, the front-desk receptionist at a small community clinic. You don't like stopping every minute to type. You let status changes pile up and enter them all at once, roughly every 10 to 15 minutes.

When a patient asks "how long?", you ignore the computer and give your own number from a quick look at the waiting room. You tend to count walk-ins as quicker than they are, so your guesses for walk-ins usually come out about a third too low.

When the system suggests putting a late patient back on the schedule, you usually just say fine.
